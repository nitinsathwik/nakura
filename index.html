Who We Are

India's Trusted UCO Aggregator

Nakura Industries Private Limited is a specialized Used Cooking Oil (UCO) aggregation company operating across India. We collect waste cooking oil from restaurants, hotels, canteens, and food establishments — turning a disposal problem into a clean energy solution.

Our supply chain connects food-service businesses with biodiesel and SAF (Sustainable Aviation Fuel) producers, ensuring premium quality, traceability, and compliance with all regulatory standards.

Why It Matters

The UCO Advantage

🌿

Cleaner Emissions

Biodiesel from UCO reduces greenhouse gas emissions by up to 80% compared to fossil diesel, helping India meet its carbon reduction targets.

♻️

Circular Economy

We prevent waste cooking oil from entering drains and water bodies — transforming a pollution risk into a valuable, sustainable resource.

📋

Regulatory Compliance

FSSAI mandates UCO disposal guidelines for food businesses. We make compliance easy, seamless, and even revenue-generating for our partners.

💰

Earn From Waste

Partner restaurants receive fair compensation for their UCO. Turn waste into income with zero operational burden — we handle everything.

🛡️

Certified & Traceable

Full chain-of-custody documentation ensures our UCO meets the highest international quality and sustainability certifications.

🚀

SAF-Ready Quality

Our premium UCO meets Sustainable Aviation Fuel feedstock standards — powering not just road vehicles, but the future of green aviation.

What We Offer

Our Services

🍴

Restaurant UCO Collection

Free scheduled collection from restaurants, QSRs, cloud kitchens, hotels & institutional kitchens. We supply containers, logistics, and paperwork at zero cost to you.

🏭

Biodiesel Plant Supply

Consistent, high-volume UCO supply to biodiesel manufacturers with tested FFA, moisture, and impurity levels meeting B20/B100 production standards.

✈️

SAF Feedstock Supply

Premium quality UCO for Sustainable Aviation Fuel production — compliant with ASTM and EU RED II standards for aviation-grade biofuel feedstock.

📊

Compliance & Documentation

Complete FSSAI-compliant UCO disposal certificates, chain of custody records, and sustainability reporting for restaurants and corporates.
